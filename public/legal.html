Terms & Conditions Privacy Policy

This document explains the rules for using Learnza and how we handle your information. It applies to every person who uses the platform — school-registered students, lecturers, school administrators, applicants, and independent (individually-registered) learners. Please read it before creating an account.

Part 1Terms & Conditions

1. Acceptance of these Terms

By creating an account, applying for admission, or otherwise using Learnza ("the Platform", "we", "us"), you agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions and the Privacy Policy below. If you do not agree, please do not use the Platform.

2. What Learnza is

Learnza is an online learning platform that serves two kinds of learners:

  • School-affiliated users — students, lecturers and administrators of a partner school, accessed via an access code or admin account issued by that school.
  • Independent learners — self-registered users studying on their own, with courses they create themselves.

Depending on your account type, the Platform may provide: an AI Teacher (an interactive, AI-generated lecturer with narrated lessons and an optional video avatar), a free e-Library, study groups, CBT-style tests and mock exams, a Digital Lab of simulated practicals, live and recorded classes, an AI Research Assistant, progress tracking and a leaderboard, digital records and a Digital ID, and — for school users — admissions, staff/student directories, attendance and results management.

3. Eligibility and your account

  • You must provide accurate, current information when registering and keep it up to date.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for all activity under your account. Tell us immediately if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.
  • Accounts are personal to you. Sharing your login with another person, or using a school access code you were not issued, is not permitted.
  • Learnza is built for higher-institution study (university, polytechnic, and college of education level). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

4. Admissions and applications

If you apply for admission through Learnza, you confirm that every document and detail you submit (including your O-level result and passport photograph) is genuine and belongs to you. Submitting false or altered documents may result in your application being rejected, or an existing admission being revoked, and may be reported to the relevant school. Applying does not guarantee acceptance — admission decisions are made solely by the school you applied to.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Core features (the e-Library, study groups and CBT practice) are free for every student. The interactive AI Teacher, recorded lecture videos and live classes are part of a paid subscription (currently ₦10,000/month or ₦105,000/year, where enforced) — pricing is shown in the app and may change with reasonable notice. During any period the app marks as a free testing phase, subscription-gated features may be temporarily available at no cost. Where a payment provider is connected, all payments are processed by that third-party provider; Learnza does not store your card details.

  • Interfere with, scrape, reverse-engineer, or attempt to gain unauthorized access to the Platform or other users' accounts or data;
  • Upload malicious files or content you do not have the right to share.

We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these Terms.

7. AI-generated content

AI Teacher lessons, generated assignments/tests, Research Assistant answers, and Digital Lab practicals are produced by third-party AI models (currently Google Gemini). While we aim for accuracy, AI-generated content can contain mistakes or become temporarily unavailable if the AI provider is overloaded or rate-limited. It is provided as a study aid, not a substitute for your school's own accredited instruction — always verify anything important with a qualified lecturer or official course material.

8. Live classes and recordings

Lecturers may host live classes and may record them for enrolled students to watch back later. By joining a live class, you consent to your participation (including audio/video where applicable) being part of that recording, which is stored and made available to your class.

Part 2Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: full name, email, phone number, password (stored hashed, never in plain text), and — for individual learners — institution type, school/course of study and level.
  • Admission & academic details: for applicants, your O-level result and passport photograph; for enrolled students, course registration, attendance, scores, results, transcript and clearance requests.
  • Usage & activity data: assessment and aptitude test answers, study group messages, AI Teacher session topics and transcripts, live class recordings, lab/practical activity, and notification history.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps) collected automatically to keep the Platform secure and working.

2. How we use your information

We use it to operate and personalize the Platform (e.g. generating AI Teacher lessons for the topics you ask about), process school admissions, let school admins run their own academic/administrative records, send you notifications, keep the service secure, and improve Learnza over time.

3. Who we share it with

  • Your school — if you're a school-affiliated user, relevant academic and administrative data is visible to that school's lecturers/admins as described above.
  • AI & video providers — the content of your AI Teacher and Research Assistant requests is sent to our AI provider (currently Google Gemini) to generate a response, and to our video-avatar provider (Simli) when you use the video avatar. These providers process it solely to return that response.
  • Service providers — email/SMS delivery, cloud hosting (Railway) and, once connected, a payment processor for subscriptions.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on secured cloud infrastructure, with passwords hashed and uploaded files (results, passport photos, recordings) kept on persistent storage tied to your account and school where applicable. We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service (e.g. a school's academic records) and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your account and associated personal data at any time, subject to records a school is required to keep for its own compliance.

6. Your rights

Subject to the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise any of these, use the contact details below.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local/session storage to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences (like dark mode) — not third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

8. International transfers

Because our AI and hosting providers operate infrastructure outside Nigeria, some data (specifically, the content of AI requests and hosted application data) may be processed on servers abroad, under those providers' own security safeguards.

9. Children's privacy

Learnza is built for higher-institution learners. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Platform evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

11. Contact us

Questions about these Terms or your data? Reach your school's administrator (for school-related records), or contact Learnza support through the channel provided in the app.
